What is OffStudy?

YOUR DIGITAL
STUDY DESK.

OffStudy is your personal digital workspace for keeping notes, PDFs, assignments and study resources organized and accessible whenever you need them.

No more digging through folders, email attachments or scattered cloud drives. Every piece of material you upload is stored right here — organized by subject, searchable in seconds, and available with a single click.

Built entirely with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, OffStudy stores your files locally in your browser using IndexedDB — meaning your data stays private, fast, and offline-ready.

How It Works

SIMPLE.
POWERFUL.
PRIVATE.

OffStudy is designed to get out of your way and let you focus on learning. Three steps, and you're organized.

📤
Step 1 — Upload
Drag and drop or browse to upload PDFs, Word docs, presentations, images, and text files up to 50MB.
🗂️
Step 2 — Organize
Tag each note with a subject. Filter, sort and search your entire library in real time.
📖
Step 3 — Study
View PDFs and images directly in the browser. Download any file. Delete what you don't need.
Built With

ZERO DEPENDENCIES.

OffStudy is built entirely with web standards — no frameworks, no libraries, no tracking. Just clean, fast, modern HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript IndexedDB localStorage IntersectionObserver Web Blob API CSS Grid CSS Flexbox CSS Variables Space Grotesk Font
🔒 PRIVACY FIRST

All your data — files, notes, metadata — is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. Your study material is completely private.
